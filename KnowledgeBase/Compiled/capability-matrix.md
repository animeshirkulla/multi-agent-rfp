# Product Capability Matrix

_Canonical, de-duplicated list of what the product(s) can do, by functional domain. Built from Solution documents only. Cite the source key(s) per capability._

## Permitting & Record Management
- **Unified all-in-one permitting platform** — single system of record spanning licensing, planning, building, fire, and code enforcement, covering the full permit lifecycle (intake → review → approval → issuance → inspection scheduling → closure). _Sources: A3-Kitsap, A2-Calgary, A7-Montgomery, A8-Johnson_
- **Configurable case-based intake for all regulatory case types** — land use/planning applications, subdivisions, development permits, building permits, right-of-way permits, variances, and other specialized approvals via configurable case types. _Sources: A2-Calgary, A3-Kitsap_
- **Flexible record types for non-permit decisions** — variances, appeals, and pre-application conferences handled in the same platform. _Sources: A3-Kitsap_
- **Application capture & milestone tracking** — capture application data, track status/milestones, and surface the requirements needed to advance a record. _Sources: A7-Montgomery, A8-Johnson_
- **Project-level / parent-child management** — group related permits/licenses under a parent/child project umbrella; parent/child parcel and permit relationships with closure contingencies (a child cannot close without parent approval). _Sources: A3-Kitsap, A4-Arlington, A8-Johnson_
- **Multiple independently-tracked due-date types per permit** — review due, expiration, and response deadlines. _Sources: A3-Kitsap_
- **Configurable expiration, extension & reactivation** — auto-expiration by date/inactivity/failure-to-correct with grace periods and notices, expiring-permit reports, and extension/reactivation request workflows; auto-extend expiration based on inspection activity. _Sources: A3-Kitsap, A7-Montgomery, A8-Johnson_
- **Amendment/revision handling** — revise a permit while preserving the original permit number and issuance date. _Sources: A7-Montgomery_
- **Conditions of approval & auto-progression** — conditions tied to workflow checkpoints with auto-progression/closure when met; unique fees per application type. _Sources: A7-Montgomery, A2-Calgary_
- **Multiple contacts per record** — multiple primary contacts, each with distinct roles and notification preferences; unlimited contacts per case retained with full history. _Sources: A3-Kitsap, A8-Johnson_
- **Multi-structure & subdivision handling** — individual or grouped permits per structure; multi-parcel subdivision recognition. _Sources: A1-Sedgwick_
- **Permit, parcel & address holds** — staff block further action on a permit or block issuance for a parcel/address. _Sources: A1-Sedgwick_
- **Address/parcel integration on intake** — addresses selected from GIS (manual entry prohibited), auto-associated to a parcel; supports address-less permits (e.g., right-of-way). _Sources: A7-Montgomery, A8-Johnson_
- **User-defined numbering & ID schemes** — configurable alphanumeric ID schemes for project/application/permit/certificate; project priority flags. _Sources: A7-Montgomery, A5-Austin, A10-Texas_
- **Complete parcel/property history view** — applications, permits, licenses, inspections, notes, complaints, code enforcement, and parcel changes in one view. _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **Site plan / photo / document attachment to records** — customers and staff upload and link files to permit records. _Sources: A1-Sedgwick_
- **Contextual GIS/enforcement display on the record** — GIS data, code-enforcement issues, and stop-work orders shown on the permit record. _Sources: A7-Montgomery_
- **Zoning & land-use case management** — dedicated Planning module for zoning/land use/development review, pre-application meetings, automated zoning-application routing, public-notice tracking, zoning board decisions/appeals, decision letters, and zoning application packets (ZAPs). _Sources: A8-Johnson, A1-Sedgwick, A4-Arlington_
- **Plat review workflow** — Planning & Zoning plat review with surveyor response upload, signature checklist, and digital/scanned plat document management. _Sources: A8-Johnson_

- **Waste-hauler vehicle licensing** — VIN/make/model storage, trash vs. recycle type, prorated fee calculation, bulk vehicle upload (.xlsx/.csv/.tsv), VIN de-duplication, and printable vehicle-display documents. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Background-check & criminal-history workflows** — pass/fail/pending verification with auto-flag for manual review and review queues; criminal history evaluation letters linked to license type. _Sources: A10-Texas_
- **Experience audits** — applicant experience logs (hours/date/location), supervisor relationship and license-validity verification, random selection, and audit history. _Sources: A10-Texas_
- **List requests** — configurable record types (with/without fees) filterable by type/board/county, results downloadable/emailable as PDF or CSV. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **Record merge for duplicate license holders.** _Sources: A10-Texas_
- **License document/badge generation** — generate license documents and badges from templates; batch and individual print-room printing with audit-ID tracking. _Sources: A8-Johnson, A9_Pennsylvania_
- **Accounts Receivable tied to licensing.** _Sources: A11_
