## Integrations & Technical Fit
- **City systems integrations (all "Yes, through integration", priced into

## Known Limitations / Caveats
- **Integration from RSI Government Premier** — marked **No** natively; "Priced
  into implementation" (i.e., requires implementation effort, not out-of-box).
- **Light configuration** items require setup based on prorate business rules:
  pass-through fees, proration logic, late fee/penalty escalation, interest
  charges, delinquent account workflows, penalty & interest calculation, date
  triggers & notifications, escalation rules, branching logic.
- **City system integrations** (STORM, PeopleSoft, Government Premier, Kubra,
  ECM/SharePoint, etc.) are delivered "through integration" and priced into
  implementation — not native/turnkey.

## Positioning / Differentiators
- Strong native coverage across licensing lifecycle, inspections, document
  management, portal, and reporting (majority of requirements answered "native").
- No-code/low-code admin configuration positioned as enabling City staff
  self-service without vendor/development services.
- ADA/WCAG 2 and multi-language compliant customer portal.
- Enhanced Reporting Database (ERD) for data export/format flexibility.
