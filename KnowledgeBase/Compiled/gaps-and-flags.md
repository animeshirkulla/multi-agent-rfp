# Gaps, Limitations & Red Flags

_Known product limitations and constraints, drawn from Solution documents. These are what the fit-analyst screens a candidate proposal against._

## Product Limitations
- **Route optimization not native** — inspector route calculation / most-efficient routing is not built in; inspectors use device navigation apps and a 3rd-party route optimizer (CSV export), with an AI partner offered at extra cost. A8-Johnson also cannot do real-time route updates from inspector location (365), routing on cluster maps (368), or configurable colored map pins (369). Matters for inspection-heavy RFPs mandating built-in routing. _Sources: A10-Texas, A4-Arlington, A5-Austin, A8-Johnson_
- **One inspector per inspection / no inspection groups** — inspection types assign to individual inspectors, not groups; only a single inspector can be assigned per inspection. Watch RFPs mandating multi-inspector or group-based assignment. _Sources: A7-Montgomery, A5-Austin_
- **No inspection waitlists** — online scheduling shows available dates/times but does not support waitlists. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Finalized inspection reports not on the customer portal** — customers see inspection notes/statuses online, but the finalized report is emailed rather than posted (347, "Not Available", high priority). _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **No estimated inspector arrival time on the portal** — ETA can be sent to customers only via Accela Mobile text; not displayed on the online portal. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Cannot delete an in-progress mobile inspection on the device** — inspectors can only mark Cancel; batch/admin delete required. Ref 518. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **No offline access to code references** — code references are unavailable in offline mode (341, "Not Available"). _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **Electronic signature via attestation only** — provided as attestation text / a disclaimer checkbox, not a full certificate-based digital signature; ESRA-compliant linked e-signature requires DocuSign (not included in pricing). Watch RFPs mandating digital/certificate-based e-signatures. _Sources: A10-Texas, A5-Austin, A4-Arlington_
- **Native plan review depends on ProjectDox; no measurement/unit-conversion calculator; no same-day plan-review waitlist** — the bulk of plan-review capability is delivered via Avolve ProjectDox; neither Accela nor ProjectDox provides a unit-conversion calculator, PDF conversion, or same-day plan-review reservation/waitlist. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Applicant markup of approved plans not supported** — markup tools are for reviewers; applicant markup of approved plans is stated as not provided. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Native AI plan review not asserted** — the AI-in-plan-review question was answered with the Digital Plan Room and third-party integration, not a native AI capability. Watch RFPs mandating AI-assisted plan review. _Sources: A2-Calgary_
- **ePermitHub Digital Plan Room future items** — count tool for hydrants/fixtures (EPR.30), markups combining annotations with calibration (EPR.47), and offline field/mobile work (EPR.57) are future-release, not currently available. _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **No weighting/complexity scoring for application/document/plan-review types** — weighting exists only for inspection types; no reviewer plan-weight/complexity workload metric. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Online portal cannot process multiple tender types in one transaction** — the back office can; the citizen portal cannot split a payment across tender types, and more than one payment method in a single transaction is "Not Available" (439). _Sources: A5-Austin, A8-Johnson_
- **No recurring/automatic payments** — recurring or auto-pay is not an option on the portal (422, "Not Available"). _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **No payment chargeback dispute handling** — the system does not support handling/disputing chargebacks (450, "Not Available"). _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **No per-DAO invoice/receipt template customization** — DAOs cannot customize invoice/receipt designs (448, "Not Available"). _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **Fee-schedule configuration not via GUI** — configured via config tables rather than a graphical UI. Ref 330. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **Workflows have no effective/expiration dates** — workflows can be versioned but not assigned effective/end dates, and only one workflow can be active at a time (contrast: business rules do support effective/expiration dating). _Sources: A5-Austin, A10-Texas_
- **Post-submission applicant edits restricted** — after submission, applicants can edit only via a formal amendment; free editing is limited to configurable pre-submission windows. _Sources: A3-Kitsap_
- **No drag-and-drop file upload** — users select single/multiple files instead; a roadmap enhancement not slated for a release. Refs 398 (A9), GT.31/PP.60 (A4). _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania, A4-Arlington_
- **Bulk scanning not supported** — multi-file upload is supported, but bulk/direct scanning is not (scan to desktop then upload). Ref 389 (A9), GT.33 (A4). _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania, A4-Arlington_
- **No QA checks for imported images** — quality-assurance checks on imported images are "Not Available" (567). _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **No search inside document content** — search covers document name/metadata only, not text within documents. Refs 406, 396. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **No native report-library metadata** — report version/owner is not natively maintained; workaround is embedding version/owner in the report name. Ref 364. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **No "suggested searches" / autocomplete** — partial/wildcard search is supported, but no suggested-search options when a query returns no results and no dynamic autocomplete for data-entry fields (133). _Sources: A10-Texas, A5-Austin, A8-Johnson_
- **No internal chat or real-time messaging/broadcast** — internal staff communication is phone and email only; no messaging/broadcasting to predefined user groups. Refs 129 (A9). _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania, A5-Austin_
- **Many-to-one dialogue communications feature not supported** — attested "No" (no explanation given). Ref 95. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **Notification-type selection limited** — users can select a delivery method but not which types of communications they receive (cannot disable regulation-required messages). Ref 309. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **No self-service email/contact update without login** — login is required for any contact/email update, for security. Refs 97, 99. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **User status for routing not supported** — cannot let users set an availability status (available/lunch/OOO); calendar functionality only. Ref 253. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **GIS maps require login; read-only authoritative GIS data** — users cannot view GIS maps or run map-based queries without logging in, and Accela references but cannot update authoritative address/parcel data (parcel genealogy only via APO). A4-Arlington also answered "No" to a publicly accessible GIS viewer (PP.73), real-time GIS property inquiry by address (PP.72), and public spatial queries (PP.76). Watch RFPs requiring public/anonymous map viewing or GIS write-back. _Sources: A10-Texas, A4-Arlington, A5-Austin_
- **Public portal feature gaps** — A4-Arlington answered "No" to native multi-language (browser Google Translate only, PP.4), public subscription notifications by City criteria (PP.74), public-comment collection on planning cases (PP.77), posting public-access documents for download (PP.79), and time-based form availability (PP.16). Watch public-engagement-heavy RFPs. _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **Language-access services tracking not supported** — cannot collect/track language-access services provided to limited-English community members. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Licensing single-screen view gap** — cannot view/print all forms for an account plus related accounts from one screen; only from within the record (LC.8). _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **Batch document print/generation gaps** — printing multiple selected existing documents (122b) and generating multiple documents from a template for a filtered group (122c) are "Not Available." _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **Work-order UI gaps** — drag-and-drop management of inspection work orders on a calendar/list view (308) and color-coding/visual status indicators (310) are "Not Available." _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **Global (not per-type) max document upload size** — maximum upload size is a single system-wide setting, not configurable per document type. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **No in-application data encryption** — encryption is at-rest and in-transit only; application-layer encryption answered "No" (GT.50). _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **Audit-log retention/export gaps** — no admin-configurable audit-log retention duration (GT.76) and no automated audit-log export (GT.80, export is manual). _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **No native SIEM logging integration** — "Accela does not natively integrate with SIEM" (521). Watch RFPs mandating SIEM log forwarding. _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **No approve-from-within-email workflow** — cannot approve/record a workflow step directly from an email (GT.135). _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **No multi-tab / split-screen / side-by-side record viewing** — concurrent sessions/environments require different browsers (GT.102/103); no split-screen on dual monitors (G-1.39); the web interface cannot open records in separate tabs (workaround: record pinning/quick access). _Sources: A4-Arlington, A7-Montgomery, A3-Kitsap_
- **No map redline save-as-document / no back-office georegistration** — maps can be marked up but not saved as a separate PDF (GT.163); georegistration needs ArcMap/ArcGIS Pro, not the back office (GT.165). _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **No on-request system performance reports** — a SaaS Availability report is available via Support, but performance reports are not provided. _Sources: A7-Montgomery_
- **No user-defined effective-date deployment of updates** — for SaaS, Accela controls upgrades on a fixed monthly release window; the agency cannot set its own effective dates. _Sources: A7-Montgomery_
- **AI/ML capabilities are roadmap, not confirmed GA** — ML assistance for workflow/basic Q&A is on "Accela's current AI roadmap," and the Accela 311 AI-based CRM is slated for a future release (Dec 2025); current availability is ambiguous. _Sources: A3-Kitsap, A8-Johnson_
- **SSO/MFA is roadmap, not GA (A10-Texas)** — single sign-on with MFA is planned via Okta but not yet available in that response. Watch RFPs mandating SSO/MFA at go-live. _Sources: A10-Texas_
- **Thin/generic "understands and can comply" attestations** — many requirements (multi-jurisdiction support, data history retention, data migration, OnBase/Laserfiche/ATPro/Aumentum interfaces, GIS/heat-mapping, MAPD data search/custom reporting, public reporting) were answered generically with no mechanism described, implying configuration/integration effort rather than demonstrated out-of-box capability. Do not overclaim these as proven native features. _Sources: A1-Sedgwick_

## Constraints / Watch-Outs
- **SMS/text notifications require a 3rd-party integration** — text messaging is delivered only via a 3rd party (Selectron, Twilio via Communication Manager, or an SMS gateway), not natively; IVR inspection scheduling likewise routes through Selectron. _Sources: A10-Texas, A4-Arlington, A5-Austin, A3-Kitsap, A1-Sedgwick_
- **Guided decision-tree / "Select a Service" intake requires an add-on or integration** — met via OpenCities integration (A10-Texas, flagged partly future release) or the optional Premium Citizen Experience add-on (A7-Montgomery); base Citizen Access provides only the portal. (A5-Austin attests it natively.) _Sources: A10-Texas, A7-Montgomery_
- **Select-a-Service wizard has data-retrieval limits** — the wizard does not retrieve existing permits or publicly available site/subdivision data without further integration. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Plan review / Bluebeam packaging** — the Digital Plan Room (ePermitHub) is a priced add-on (in A4-Arlington most EPR requirements were answered "T"/third-party, fully met but priced in Attachment C1); a direct Bluebeam integration incurs additional services cost if ePermitHub is not used. Budget/scope EPR separately for plan-review-heavy RFPs. _Sources: A4-Arlington, A8-Johnson_
- **Markup/redaction requires a third-party tool.** Ref 403. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **24x7 AI chatbot only via third party** — the generative chatbot is delivered through a third-party integration. Ref 487. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **Barcode scanning / OCR require integration** — barcode scanning and OCR-to-field extraction need third-party integration. Refs 109, 390. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **External Legal-system routing requires an API integration** — routing work items to a separate Legal system needs an API integration. Ref 262. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **Reporting-DB change tracking is limited** — audit of changes in the reporting database is supported "with limitations." Ref 354. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **External-table reporting needs the optional ERD** — reporting against tables external to the core system requires the separately procured Enhanced Reporting Database. _Sources: A7-Montgomery_
- **Self-service report authoring not fully specified** — reporting/analytics is a core capability, but one response explicitly notes it does not spell out the exact self-service report-builder mechanism. Avoid overclaiming no-code end-user report authoring. _Sources: A2-Calgary_
- **Many requirements met via configuration rather than out-of-the-box** — renewals, auto-approval criteria, list requests, report scheduling, worklists, notification scheduling, and similar require configuration; some items (end-dating comments per review cycle, intra-day inspection reassignment, auto-notify next-on-route, rich-text inspection findings, cluster mapping) require configuration plus custom scripting. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania, A3-Kitsap, A8-Johnson_
- **Real-time estimated review times require custom development** — portal display of estimated review times needs a custom dashboard. _Sources: A3-Kitsap_
- **Real-time emergency GIS data requires custom integration** — road closures/escape routes depend on configuration and custom integration with county mapping systems. _Sources: A3-Kitsap_
- **Cannot ingest raw AutoCAD for GIS automation directly** — GIS automation is via Esri REST integration, not raw AutoCAD file intake. _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Customer-initiated refund requests handled as an amendment record** — not a dedicated native refund-request flow. _Sources: A3-Kitsap_
- **Fee/penalty edge cases require light configuration** — pass-through fees, proration logic, late-fee/penalty escalation, and interest charges are not fully out-of-box; they need configuration on prorate business rules. _Sources: A11_
- **Workflow automation edge cases require light configuration** — date triggers & notifications, escalation rules, and branching logic need configuration. _Sources: A11_
- **Enforcement calculations require light configuration** — delinquent-account workflows and penalty & interest calculation need configuration. _Sources: A11_
- **NSF, bonds, grants & records-retention delivered via configuration/policy** — NSF handling (449), bond (462) and grant (463) management are configured, not out-of-box modules; Kansas Statutes retention (570), retention-schedule application (571), self-service retention config (576), and subset image destruction (569) are handled via Accela's Data Storage Policy/scripting. _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **Duplicate-contact prevention needs scripting** — preventing/resolving duplicate contact records requires business-rule configuration/scripting, not native de-duplication (103). _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **No record locking (collaborative live-edit model)** — the system does not prevent concurrent editing; it flags/banners records under review rather than hard-locking (142, 162). Watch RFPs mandating exclusive record locking. _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **No robust native administrative-hearing module** — hearing scheduling/attendance/votes are handled via general features rather than a dedicated hearing module (404); A5-Austin similarly only attaches hearing checklists to a meeting. _Sources: A8-Johnson, A5-Austin_
- **Elavon / credit-card integration requires modification** — needs the Forte Payment Gateway (A7) or a gateway/partner custom service (A8) to complete the Elavon integration. _Sources: A7-Montgomery, A8-Johnson_
- **Web/in-person payments via the Velosimo connector (priced)** — US Bank and Elavon integrations are met through the priced Velosimo service, not natively. _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **Financial-module integrations are custom / added-cost** — interfaces for inspection fees (352) and citations/fines (402) are custom integrations; e-check bank-account validation (442), Oracle ERP import/export (519), automated data-quality reports (477), and data-conflict resolution during sync (537) are added cost. _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **City / named integrations are implementation-priced, not turnkey** — STORM, PeopleSoft, Government Premier (RSI Government Premier inbound answered "No" natively), Kubra, and ECM/SharePoint are all delivered "through integration" and priced into implementation. _Sources: A11_
- **Many named integrations carry per-interface cost** — GIS (Esri/Survey123), Laserfiche, Workday ERP, state license validations (TDLR/TABC/TSBPE/TDI), county appraisal/recorder, OneView/OpenCounter 311, Tyler Incode court, Intergraph CAD, Cartegraph EAM, Qualtrics, Comm100, and utility feeds all price individually; integration-heavy scope drives cost. _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **Third-party integration fees borne by the agency** — Elavon (518), CE/LMS validation (517), CentralSquare EAM (525), and CiviCRM/future CRM (526) integrations carry agency-responsible third-party fees. _Sources: A8-Johnson_
- **Integration-dependent capabilities (A1-Sedgwick)** — IVR requires Selectron; payment processing requires MSB/MUNIS (Tyler) and SAP S/4HANA via RFC; public contractor license verification may require a state licensing-board integration. Effort/cost for integration-heavy RFPs. _Sources: A1-Sedgwick_
- **Bond cash-deposit tracking requires City Finance system integration.** _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Online complaint intake requires 311/CSR integration; County/Assessor data flagging requires a County/Assessor integration.** _Sources: A5-Austin_
- **Alternative online payment methods depend on the payment provider** — Google Pay, PayPal, and Apple Pay availability is contingent on the agency's payment provider. _Sources: A7-Montgomery_
- **License-ID photo production requires modification** — integration with existing photo machines only if they expose an open API. _Sources: A7-Montgomery_
- **Virtual inspection is basic out-of-box** — config-level virtual inspection uses an agency-provided web-conferencing service; robust virtual inspection requires partner VuSpex. _Sources: A7-Montgomery_
- **Data profiling/cleansing rely on the agency's SMEs** — Accela owns mapping into Accela and is consultative, but the customer identifies data-quality issues. _Sources: A7-Montgomery_
- **Legacy data conversion not itemized per object** — conversion cost is not broken down by object; scope all legacy objects together. _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **Manual holiday-calendar maintenance & no per-user notification preferences** — holiday calendars are updated manually (SR.50); users cannot configure individual notification preferences such as daily-summary vs. day-before alert (PL.42). _Sources: A4-Arlington_
- **Cloud storage cap** — 2.5 TB per cloud customer (renewable annually); additional storage in 500 GB blocks at extra cost. _Sources: A9_Pennsylvania_
- **Internal users are seat/license-limited** — only external citizen-portal users are unlimited; internal staff are licensed per seat, and Accela SaaS is licensed per user (user growth adds cost). _Sources: A7-Montgomery, A1-Sedgwick_
- **On-site support is not included** — billed at Accela time-and-materials rates plus airfare/lodging/transportation/meals. _Sources: A1-Sedgwick_
- **Live phone support hours are limited** — roughly 6 a.m.–8 p.m. CT Mon–Fri; after-hours is online/email/community only (24/7 designated engineer only under proposed Preferred Support). Watch RFPs mandating 24/7 live phone support. _Sources: A1-Sedgwick, A8-Johnson_
- **Help tickets limited to Authorized Support Contacts** — not all agency staff can open support cases; only designated contacts (10 under Preferred Support) may submit tickets (592). _Sources: A8-Johnson, A1-Sedgwick_
- **Implementation cost scoped for a single release** — multi-phase/tailored enablement is possible but a separate collaboration; the base estimate assumes one release. _Sources: A1-Sedgwick_
- **Data exchange frequency is requirement-dependent** — supported but subject to the agency's requirements. _Sources: A11_
- **Several use cases flagged as needing clarification** — e.g., future-construction-roads display (A72), bar/QR-code smart scanning (H61/H62), retention notifications (J123), GIS reassociation (J182), and County/Assessor notification flagging (J204) were answered as needing more detail to confirm fit. _Sources: A5-Austin_
